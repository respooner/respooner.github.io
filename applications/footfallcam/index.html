Fetch every source and log it

Downloads 34 raw files from OpenDengue, NASA POWER, the Ministry of Health, OxCGRT and OpenDOSM. Each download is logged with its URL, UTC time, size and SHA-256, and raw files are never edited afterwards.

Engineering choices

  • Python standard library only, so it runs anywhere with Python 3.10+.
  • Raw files are never edited; every change happens in code.
  • A pandas version of the same cleaning produces a matching output.
  • A decision log records each judgement call, its reason and who agreed.

Project 2 · Machine learning · co-author

Cardiometabolic risk models on NHANES, 2011–2018

Two studies on US national health survey data, with Xiaonan Zhan, Yichi Zhang and Xingwei Mao. We asked which body measurements really improve a model that flags people at high cardiometabolic risk, and tested each answer on a later survey cycle the models had never been trained on.

Preprint · medRxiv · posted 12 October 2026 · not yet peer reviewed

Does handgrip strength improve cardiometabolic risk stratification beyond detailed body composition? NHANES 2011–2014 with temporal validation

Zhan X, Shen M, Zhang Y, Mao X. DOI 10.64898/2026.10.09.26365176

Companion study on NHANES 2015–2018 (visceral fat versus body fat percentage): manuscript in preparation.

My role: research planning and coordination, cardiovascular data processing, exploratory analysis, visualization, and synthesis of the findings.

2,114participants across four survey cycles
3algorithms: logistic, elastic-net, XGBoost
5 × 4nested cross-validation folds
2,000paired bootstrap resamples per comparison
10⁻¹⁵agreement when metrics were recomputed independently

Which measurements add real information?

Validation cohort: frozen models scored once on the later survey cycle.

Gain is real (95% CI above zero) No reliable gain (CI includes zero)

Change in AUC when each measurement is added, logistic regression, with 95% bootstrap intervals. Switch cohorts to see that the result holds in both.

How the models were kept honest

  • Predictors entered only through a fixed whitelist, so no outcome information leaked into the inputs.
  • Imputation, scaling and tuning were learned inside training folds only.
  • The analysis plan and every fitted pipeline were locked with SHA-256 checksums before validation.
  • Validation data were scored once, with no refitting or recalibration.

What we found

  • Visceral fat from DXA scans added a reproducible gain over BMI and waist size.
  • Grip strength added nothing: all 12 of its comparisons had intervals that included zero.
  • SHAP attributions showed that a feature can get model attention without improving accuracy.
  • Reporting followed the TRIPOD+AI checklist for prediction models.

Quality control

Seven checks run on every build of the dengue table

A count is only useful if you know when it is wrong. Results of the reference run on 20 October 2026; open a row to see what the check found.

0Raw file integrityPASS34 files match their logged SHA-256

Every raw file is hashed again and compared with the hash written to DOWNLOAD_LOG.csv at download time, so a silently changed file cannot slip in.

1Calendar completenessPASS626 consecutive weeks, 2011-01-02 to 2022-12-25

Every Sunday-start week appears exactly once, with no duplicates and no jumps in the calendar.

2Case countsPASS2 missing weeks flagged, not filled silently

Missing in the source and kept as missing, with a flag column:

  • 2014-12-28
  • 2020-12-27
3OutliersWARN5 weeks changed by more than 50% in one week

Raised as warnings for review against the WHO source. The 2013-12-29 drop is the first week taken from a different WHO source document.

  • 2011-09-04: 178 → 310 (+74%)
  • 2013-12-29: 2,087 → 1,019 (−51%)
  • 2014-01-05: 1,019 → 1,930 (+89%)
  • 2014-08-03: 1,818 → 2,975 (+64%)
  • 2017-11-19: 1,003 → 1,536 (+53%)
4Climate rangesPASS75,968 location-days within plausible limits

Daily temperature, rainfall and humidity at 16 state capitals checked against physical limits before weekly aggregation.

5Lag alignmentPASSRandom weeks recomputed by an independent code path

Five random weeks, one random lag each, three variables. Examples:

  • 2015-02-22 temperature lag 1: stored 25.6847, recomputed 25.6847
  • 2019-08-18 rainfall lag 4: stored 38.2796, recomputed 38.2796

Why FootfallCam

The same problems, at a shop entrance

FootfallCam counts visitors with 3D sensors and AI video analytics for retailers, malls, libraries and airports. The data problems I worked on are the ones a people-counting platform meets every day.

In my projectsAt a people-counting company
