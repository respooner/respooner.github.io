What the code shows

Talking points

  • Data-driven design. Cards, enemies, jokers, relics and perks are plain objects with small hook functions; the engines never mention a specific item.
  • Algorithms. Poker hand evaluation, recursive shadowcasting, BFS distance maps, non-crossing random-walk map generation, Fisher–Yates shuffles.
  • Game feel. Async animation sequencing, tweened movement, hit-flash, particles, screen shake and floating numbers, all without a framework.
  • Product thinking. Telegraphed enemy intents, live damage previews, keyboard and touch controls, reduced-motion support and saved best scores.
