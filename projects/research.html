Methods, contribution scope and the materials currently public for the two research projects.

Malaysia dengue data pipeline

Scope: data collection, weekly alignment and quality control for the 2011–2022 preparation phase of a research project.

  • Work described: merge five open sources; preserve raw files; log download hashes; check missing weeks, outliers, climate ranges and lag alignment.
  • Reported output: 626 weeks, 34 raw files and seven checks. The homepage shows a reference run dated 20 October 2026, including warnings and missingness.
  • Reproduction: the project describes Python 3.10+, download.py → pipeline.py → qc.py → compare_with_reference.py. These scripts and their run artifacts are not distributed in this repository, so these are methodology notes rather than a runnable research release.
  • Evidence boundary: the chart and QC summaries are presentation content. Their presence here does not independently reproduce the source analysis.

NHANES cardiometabolic risk models

Collaborative research. The portfolio describes research planning and coordination, cardiovascular data processing, exploratory analysis, visualization and synthesis as Shen Majun's contribution.

  • Methods presented: logistic regression, elastic-net and XGBoost; nested cross-validation; temporal validation; paired bootstrap intervals; leakage controls.
  • Public manuscript: the linked medRxiv preprint credits Zhan X, Shen M, Zhang Y and Mao X. It is not yet peer reviewed. A companion study is described as a manuscript in preparation.
  • Reproduction: analysis code, cohort-building scripts and model artifacts are not included in this website repository. Refer to the manuscript for the study's reported design; the website chart is a presentation layer.
